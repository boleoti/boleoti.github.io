Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as your display name and avatar) from TikTok. No other personal data is collected.

2. How we use it

This information is used only to publish the videos you choose to your own TikTok account and to show which account is connected.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok in order to publish your content.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only by the app. They are deleted when you disconnect the app or ask us to delete them.

5. Your rights

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings, and you can ask for your data to be deleted by contacting us.
