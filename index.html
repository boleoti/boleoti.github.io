Chris Gaming News

Chris Gaming News is a personal publishing tool used by its creator to share GTA gaming news, gameplay clips and highlights on his own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. Videos prepared by the owner are uploaded to TikTok through the Content Posting API.
  3. The owner chooses the caption and privacy settings before each post is published.

The app only posts content to the account that authorized it. It does not read, collect or sell data from other TikTok users.
